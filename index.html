01 — About

About me

I’m a Computer Science student at Georgia Tech, concentrating in Intelligence and Cybersecurity.

Those two threads chase the questions I find hardest to put down: how a system learns something nobody explicitly taught it, and how that same system holds up when a person is actively trying to break it. The overlap between them — models that have to be trustworthy, not just accurate — is where I want to spend my time.

Right now I’m working through object-oriented design in CS 2340, which means a lot of time thinking about how to structure software so that the next person to open the file isn’t cursing my name. The project below is the result.

Away from a terminal I’m usually chasing down dance music, petting a dog that did not ask to be petted, or eating an unreasonable amount of sushi.
